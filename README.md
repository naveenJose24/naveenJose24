## About me

Senior Flutter Developer with **5+ years** of experience shipping cross-platform apps with Flutter, React Native and the Android SDK.

- 📱 Production apps live on **Google Play** and the **App Store**, across HealthTech, Fintech, EdTech, Commerce and Communication
- 🧱 Clean Architecture with **BLoC / Cubit**, **Provider** and **GetX**, plus Freezed for immutable models
- 🤖 AI-powered features: in-app assistants, LLM-generated content and text-to-speech
- 💳 Payment integrations: **Stripe**, **Razorpay**, **Tamara**, in-app purchases and **WalletConnect**
- ⚡ Real-time systems: **WebSockets**, **XMPP**, **Socket.IO** and **WebRTC**
- 🚀 Delivery: Jenkins CI/CD and Shorebird over-the-air updates
